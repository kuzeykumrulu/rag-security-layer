Company Communication and Meetings Policy (2026):

Company business is conducted on approved channels: company email, the
company chat workspace, and the company video conferencing tool. Work
discussions should not move to personal messaging services, because those
records fall outside the retention schedule and outside the company's
security controls.

Email is the channel of record for anything that needs to be found later —
decisions, commitments to external parties, and anything a colleague joining
later would need to reconstruct.

Chat is for coordination rather than decisions. A decision reached in chat
should be written up in the relevant project record or by email the same day.

There is no expectation of a response outside working hours. Messages sent
outside working hours do not carry an implied deadline, and no employee is
expected to monitor channels while on leave.

Meetings must have a stated purpose and an agenda circulated in advance.
A meeting invitation without either may be declined without explanation.

Recurring meetings are reviewed every quarter by their organiser, who is
expected to cancel any that no longer justify the time.

Meetings are recorded only with the agreement of everyone present. Recordings
are retained for 90 days. Where a meeting covers Confidential information, the
recording carries the same classification as the material discussed.

External participants may be invited to internal meetings by the organiser,
who is responsible for confirming that nothing Confidential will be discussed
in their presence, or for arranging that they join only for the relevant part.

Meeting rooms seating more than eight people are booked at least one working
day in advance through the calendar system.
