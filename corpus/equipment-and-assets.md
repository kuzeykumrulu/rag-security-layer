Company Equipment and Asset Policy (2026):

Every employee is issued a company laptop on their first day. Standard issue
is a 14-inch machine; roles with a documented need for additional compute may
request a workstation-class device through their department head.

All issued equipment is recorded in the asset register against the employee's
name. Employees are responsible for the equipment assigned to them until it
is formally returned and the register is updated.

Requests for additional peripherals — external monitors, docking stations,
headsets — go through the IT service desk. Standard peripherals are fulfilled
without approval. Anything above 300 EUR requires department head approval
before IT will order it.

Equipment refresh runs on a three-year cycle. Employees are contacted by IT
when their device becomes eligible. A device may be replaced earlier if it
fails and repair is not economical.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT service desk immediately
and in no case later than 24 hours after the loss is noticed. A device that
held company data and cannot be recovered is treated as a security incident
and follows the incident process in the IT Security Policy.

Damaged equipment should be brought to the IT service desk rather than
repaired independently. Third-party repair voids the support arrangement and
may compromise the device's security configuration.

All equipment must be returned on or before an employee's last working day.
Return is arranged through the IT service desk, which confirms the asset
register has been cleared. Equipment is not transferred directly between
employees; it returns to IT and is reissued.

Personal software may not be installed on company devices. Requests for
additional software go through the IT service desk, which checks licensing
and security before installation.
