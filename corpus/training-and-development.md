Company Training and Development Policy (2026):

Every employee has an annual training budget of 1,500 EUR. The budget covers
courses, certifications, conference tickets, and books. It runs on the
calendar year and unused budget does not roll forward.

Training requests are submitted to the employee's manager, who approves on
the basis of relevance to the current role or an agreed development direction.
Requests above the annual budget require department head approval and a
written case.

Travel and accommodation for a conference are claimed separately under the
Expenses and Business Travel Policy and do not come out of the training
budget.

Time spent on approved training during working hours is working time and is
not deducted from any leave entitlement. Training scheduled outside working
hours is voluntary.

Mandatory training is separate from the training budget and is assigned
centrally. It currently comprises information security awareness, which is
completed annually, and data handling, which is completed on joining and
whenever the Data Handling Policy changes materially.

Mandatory training must be completed within 30 days of assignment. Managers
are notified when a member of their team passes that deadline.

Certifications paid for by the company remain valid to the individual. There
is no repayment clause attached to a certification funded from the annual
training budget.

Internal knowledge sharing — brown bag sessions, internal talks, written
guides — is encouraged and counts as development activity. Time spent
preparing an internal session is working time, and rooms for internal
sessions are booked through the calendar system on the same terms as any
other meeting.

Employees are asked to record completed training in the HR system so that
skills are visible when teams are formed for new work.
