Company Facilities and Building Access Policy (2026):

Building access is controlled by employee badge. Badges are issued by
reception on an employee's first day against a photo held in the HR system.
A lost badge must be reported to reception the same day so it can be
deactivated.

Badge access logs are retained for 12 months. Logs are accessible to the
Security team and are reviewed only in connection with a reported incident.

Offices are open from 07:00 to 20:00 on working days. Access outside those
hours requires an out-of-hours permission recorded against the badge, which a
department head requests from the Security team.

All visitors must be registered in the visitor system before arrival and must
be escorted at all times while on office premises. The employee who registered
the visitor is the escort of record. Visitors receive a temporary badge that
expires at the end of the day.

Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar system. Rooms seating more than
eight people must be booked at least one working day in advance. A room booked
and not used within fifteen minutes of the start time is released automatically.

Parking spaces are allocated by request through facilities. Availability is
limited and requests are handled in order of receipt. Electric vehicle
charging bays are for active charging only and are not general parking spaces.

Deliveries are received at reception and must be collected the same working
day. Reception does not accept personal deliveries.

Any facilities fault — heating, lighting, water, furniture — should be
reported through the facilities request form rather than to the Security team.
Faults affecting building access or safety should be reported by phone to
reception immediately.
