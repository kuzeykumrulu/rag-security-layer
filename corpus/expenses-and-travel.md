Company Expenses and Business Travel Policy (2026):

All business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30
calendar days of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after
that window require written approval from a department head before finance
will process them.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run following approval, and
finance aims to approve or reject every claim within 10 business days of
submission.

Every claim over 50 EUR must have an itemised receipt attached. Card
statements are not accepted as receipts. Claims below 50 EUR may be submitted
without a receipt but no more than three such claims per month.

Business travel must be booked at least 10 working days in advance through
the company travel desk. Bookings made with less notice require the traveller
to record the reason in the booking request. Economy class is standard for
all flights under six hours.

Accommodation is capped at 180 EUR per night in listed cities and 140 EUR
per night elsewhere. The cap includes local taxes. Travellers who exceed the
cap must attach a short justification to the claim.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed at actual cost up to 60 EUR per
day. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment is handled separately
and requires prior approval from the relevant department head, who must
record the client name and business purpose.

Mileage for personal vehicles used on company business is reimbursed at 0.30
EUR per kilometre. Parking and tolls incurred on business travel are
reimbursable with receipts.

Expenses incurred on behalf of another employee must be claimed by the person
who paid them, not by the person who benefited.
