Company Data Handling Policy (2026):

Company information is classified at one of three levels: Public, Internal,
and Confidential. Anything not explicitly marked is treated as Internal.

Public information may be shared outside the company without restriction.
Internal information may be shared with any employee but not outside the
company. Confidential information — employee records, salary data, customer
contracts, unreleased product work — may be shared only with named
individuals who require it for their work.

Employee personal data, including salary and contact details, is Confidential
in all cases. An employee may access their own record. Access to another
employee's record requires a documented business need and is granted through
HR, not by the record holder's colleagues.

Confidential information may not be copied to personal storage, personal
email, or unapproved cloud services. Where information must be sent outside
the company, it goes through the approved file transfer service, which
records the recipient and expires the link after 14 days.

Documents containing Confidential information must carry the classification
in the document itself. A document that leaves the company without a
classification marking has still left the company; the marking is a control,
not a defence.

Retention follows the schedule held by Legal. Employee records are retained
for the duration of employment plus seven years. Badge access logs are
retained for 12 months. Meeting recordings are retained for 90 days unless
attached to a project record.

Any suspected disclosure of Confidential information — a misdirected email,
an over-shared link, a document left in a public place — must be reported to
the IT Security team within one hour of discovery, on the same basis as any
other security incident.

Requests for company data from outside the company, including from people
identifying themselves as auditors, regulators, or law enforcement, are
routed to Legal without response.
