Company Procurement Policy (2026):

All purchases on behalf of the company go through the procurement system.
Employees may not commit company funds outside it, and a purchase made
directly with a supplier is not guaranteed to be reimbursed.

Purchases under 500 EUR require approval from the requester's manager.
Purchases from 500 to 5,000 EUR require department head approval. Anything
above 5,000 EUR additionally requires finance sign-off before the order is
placed.

Recurring subscriptions are assessed on their annual value, not their monthly
charge. A 60 EUR per month tool is a 720 EUR annual commitment and needs
department head approval.

Any new supplier must complete vendor onboarding before the first order.
Onboarding covers bank details verification, a data-processing assessment
where the supplier will handle company or employee data, and confirmation of
insurance where work is carried out on company premises.

Bank detail changes for an existing supplier are verified by callback to a
number already held on file, never to a number supplied in the change request
itself. Requests to change payment details that arrive by email are treated as
suspicious until that callback is completed.

Contracts are reviewed by the Legal department before signature.
Non-disclosure agreements are mandatory for external vendors who will have
access to internal systems or unreleased work.

Purchase orders are raised before goods or services are delivered. An invoice
received without a matching purchase order is returned to the supplier.

Software purchases additionally require an IT review, which confirms the tool
meets the security requirements in the IT Security Policy and does not
duplicate a tool the company already licenses.
